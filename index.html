A Crow’s Odyssey

Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Privacy Policy

A Crow’s Odyssey is a single-player game. The game does not use advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs, and we do not operate game servers or collect personal information directly.

Data stored on your device

The game stores settings, high scores, and collected game-card progress locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer. Removing the app deletes its locally stored game data.

Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, the game uses Apple’s Game Center service to submit scores and achievements. Apple may process your Game Center account details and gameplay activity, and scores or achievements may be visible to other Game Center users according to your Game Center settings. Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy: Game Center & Privacy.

Children

The game does not ask players to provide personal information. Game Center is an optional Apple service and is subject to the player’s Apple account and family settings.
